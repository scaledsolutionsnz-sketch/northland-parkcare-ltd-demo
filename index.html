Thirty years working Northland ground

Northland Parkcare has looked after parks, reserves and private properties from its Maungatapere base since 2002. The rule on every job is simple: turn up when agreed, do the work properly, leave the site tidy.

Behind that sits more than 30 years in the horticultural industry and over 20 years of council-approved contracting, including playground and skatepark maintenance across the Whangārei district. No job is considered too large or too small.

  • Locally owned and operated since 2002
  • Council-approved contractor for over 20 years
  • RPII-certified playground operational inspector
