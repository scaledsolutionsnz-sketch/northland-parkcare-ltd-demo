What we look after

From one-off landscaping jobs to year-round council and commercial contracts, the crew covers the full grounds workload.

Scheduled

Commercial mowing

Reserves, sports fields, road frontages and school grounds cut on schedule, with edges trimmed and clippings managed.

Design & build

Landscaping

Planting plans, garden builds and finishing work for sections, subdivisions and public spaces across the district.

Specialists

Tracks & walkways

New walking tracks formed, steps and boardwalks built, and existing routes cleared, drained and resurfaced.

Eco-sourced

Native planting & revegetation

Wetland, gully and reserve planting with eco-sourced natives, released and maintained until they establish.

RPII certified

Playgrounds & play equipment

Installation, repairs and certified operational inspections for councils, schools and early learning centres.

Year-round

Section clearing & property maintenance

Overgrown sections opened back up, plus hedges, spraying and ongoing grounds contracts for commercial sites.

Established2002

Care for Northland’s outdoor spaces

Northland Parkcare has looked after parks, reserves and private properties from its Whangārei base since 2002. The rule on every job is simple: turn up when agreed, do the work properly, leave the site tidy.

Our work includes parks, reserves, walking tracks and playground maintenance across the Whangārei district. Contact Paul to discuss your site and the work required.
